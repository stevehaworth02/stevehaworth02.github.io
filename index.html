About

My work in EEG-based machine learning spans seizure detection, consciousness monitoring, and neural signal classification across epilepsy, acute brain injury, coma, sleep disorders, and post-cardiac arrest populations. I build systems designed for clinical deployment. Current work includes active collaborations with UW-Madison, Harvard, Yale, and Washington University in St. Louis.

I work across CNNs, LSTMs, large language models, computer vision, statistical modeling, mathematical optimization, and the full data lifecycle, including experiment tracking and model versioning with tools like MLflow.

Research

Seizure detection under domain shift
Spatiotemporal deep learning (CNN, BiLSTM, attention) trained on scalp EEG and validated on continuous ICU recordings, with a focus on false-alarm reduction.
Seizure forecasting in the ICU
Interpretable models that estimate near-term seizure risk from continuous EEG, with external validation on independent cohorts.
Neural correlates of consciousness
Topological and spectral signatures of high-density EEG during sleep and serial awakenings.
Sensor optimization
Mixed-integer programming for minimal EEG montages that preserve classification performance.
